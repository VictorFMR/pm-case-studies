# Launch & B2B Scaling: Product-Market Fit in CPG Distribution

> **Descriptor:** *UK-based B2B Sustainable Food Import & Wholesale Scale-up*  
> **Role:** *Product Manager / Lead Product Innovation*  
> **Timeline:** *September 2016 – March 2018 (Completed / Long-Term Success)*  
> **Status:** *Fully Executed & Scaled*

---

### Confidentiality Notice
> *Organization and project names have been anonymized for discretion and confidentiality. All quantitative metrics, retailer adoption curves, supply chain data, margin structures, and strategic product decisions presented strictly reflect actual professional experience.*

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Business Context
* **Context:** A leading UK B2B wholesaler and importer specializing in ethical, organic, and sustainable food products, supplying an active network of 300+ independent retailers across the South West of the UK.
* **Business Challenge:** Face company revenue stagnation (2016–2017) and intense regional market pressure from competing distributors by discovering, validating, and launching a novel CPG category capable of driving top-line growth without violating core ethical sourcing values.
* **Project Status:** Fully executed commercial launch with sustained long-term success. The product created an entirely new market category in the UK, generating a **15% direct increase in total company revenue** and maintaining continuous sales growth for 6 consecutive years.

---

### 2. Discovery & User Research (Q3 2016 – Q1 2017)
* **Market Trend Identification (Q3 2016):** Mapped global plant-based protein innovations to address a clear market gap in unrefined, whole-ingredient meat alternatives.
* **Multi-Layered Field Research:**
  * **Retail Point-of-Sale Audit:** Partnered with the internal delivery fleet to conduct a 5-question covert survey across **114 retail client stores** during delivery drops (85% response rate).
  * **Mass Quantitative Survey:** Surveyed an end-user database of 30,000 contacts, securing **3,500 valid responses** (11.7% response rate).
* **Key Research Insights:**
  * **Rejection of Ultra-Processed Substitutes:** Conscious consumers actively rejected heavily processed soy, seitan, and TVP due to health and ingredient transparency concerns.
  * **Demand for Gastronomic Familiarity:** Buyers lacked a versatile, whole-food ingredient capable of replicating the savory, pulled texture of traditional cooked British meat dishes.
* **Ingredient Sourcing & Elimination Phase:** Evaluated and eliminated multiple international protein candidates over 3 months due to lack of organic certification, prohibitive import costs, or restrictive culinary uses before identifying green jackfruit in brine/water.

---

### 3. Strategy & Solution Definition (MVP & Unit Economics - Q2 2017)
* **Product Definition & Form Factor:** Sourced and branded young green jackfruit in brine and water as an unseasoned, shelf-stable culinary base for B2B food service and retail resale.
* **Quality Assurance & Chef Blind Testing:** Organized blind tastings with professional chefs, retail buyers, and tasting panels, achieving a **93% positive rating** for texture and sauce absorption.
* **Financial Modeling & Margin Schemes:**
  * Priced competitively slightly below the leading commercial seitan references to incentivize fast adoption.
  * Structured B2B wholesale pricing tiers to guarantee healthy gross operating margins for both the importer and independent retail accounts.

---

### 4. Execution & Go-To-Market (GTM - Q3 2017 – Q4 2017)
* **Packaging & Positional Marketing:** Designed eco-friendly B2B and retail-ready packaging with clear nutritional profiles, origin transparency, and easy-to-cook recipe guides.
* **B2B Co-Creation & Sampling Campaign:**
  * Deployed a targeted sampling strategy across key organic retail accounts and regional vegan food service clients.
  * Distributed point-of-sale (POS) displays, recipe cards, and tasting kits to support in-store customer activation.
* **Supply Chain Risk & Operations Management:**
  * **Stock Buffer Strategy:** Secured an initial 3 to 6-month inventory buffer prior to launch, later expanding to **6–9 months of stock coverage** to buffer extended maritime lead times from Asia.
  * **Supply Crisis Navigation:** Successfully absorbed major international shipping disruptions and a supplier facility fire without experiencing out-of-stock events across client stores.

---

### 5. Business Results & Impact (Q1 2018 Onward)
* **Rapid Channel Adoption Curve:**
  * **Month 1:** Adopted by 81 stores (**77.8% initial order adoption rate** among invited accounts).
  * **Month 4:** Expanded to **99% channel penetration** across the entire B2B client store network.
* **Cohort Retention Rate:** Achieved an unprecedented **97% monthly reorder rate** among adopting retail accounts over its first year.
* **Top-Line Financial Impact:**
  * Sales from this single product line directly increased total company annual revenue by **+15%**.
  * **Basket Expansion (Cross-Selling Effect):** Orders containing the product generated a **3% to 7% increase in total basket size** (*Average Order Value*) across other core wholesale catalog categories.
* **Competitive Dominance:** Blocked the primary regional competitor for 8 months. The competitor ultimately surrendered its own R&D efforts and became a sub-distributor purchasing stock directly from the co-op.

---

### 6. Retrospective & Key Learnings
* **Core Takeaways:**
  * **Validation Before Scaling:** Direct co-creation and blind testing with B2B buyers and professional chefs prior to full-scale importation was pivotal to securing a 97% recurring order rate.
  * **First-Mover Advantage:** Establishing early supply chain dominance created an unbeatable quality and price benchmark that forced main competitors into sub-distribution.
* **Self-Critical Retrospective:** The initial launch campaign underinvested in direct B2C digital marketing; a more aggressive influencer and content-led campaign at launch would have accelerated consumer pull-through even faster.
